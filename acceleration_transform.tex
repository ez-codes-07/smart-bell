\[
\mathbf{v} =
\begin{bmatrix}
a_x \\
a_y \\
a_z
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf{v}' = R\mathbf{v}.
\]

With the world $z$ axis pointing upward, the gravity-compensated acceleration is
\[
\mathbf{a}_{\text{linear}} = \mathbf{v}' -
\begin{bmatrix}
0 \\
0 \\
9.81
\end{bmatrix}
\quad \text{m/s}^2.
\]
